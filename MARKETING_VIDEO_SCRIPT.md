## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- **Color Palette**: App's brand colors (primary dark, primary blue, sunset orange)
- **Transitions**: Smooth, modern transitions
- **Text Overlays**: Clean, readable fonts
- **Screen Recordings**: High-quality app demos

### Audio
- **Background Music**: Upbeat, modern, fashion-forward
- **Voiceover**: Professional, energetic, clear
- **Sound Effects**: Subtle UI sounds, notification sounds

### Filming Requirements
- **App Screen Recordings**: Clean, high-quality demos
- **Lifestyle Footage**: Diverse models, real shopping scenarios
- **Product Shots**: Professional fashion photography
- **Delivery Scenes**: Real delivery tracking scenarios

### Platform-Specific Cuts
- **Instagram Reels/TikTok**: 15-30 seconds, vertical format
- **YouTube**: 60-90 seconds, horizontal format
- **Facebook Ads**: 30-45 seconds, square/vertical format
- **Website Hero**: 30 seconds, horizontal format

### Key Messages to Emphasize
1. **Convenience**: Shop from anywhere, anytime
2. **Speed**: Fast checkout, real-time tracking
3. **Trust**: Secure payments, verified sellers
4. **Community**: Connect with local sellers
5. **Opportunity**: Easy seller platform for entrepreneurs

### Target Audience Segments
- **Primary**: Fashion-conscious millennials (18-35)
- **Secondary**: Small business owners, fashion entrepreneurs
- **Tertiary**: Local fashion designers, boutique owners

## Production Timeline

### Pre-Production (1 week)
- Finalize script and storyboard
- Gather app screen recordings
- Plan lifestyle footage
- Cast models/actors
- Scout locations

### Production (1-2 weeks)
- Film lifestyle scenes
- Record app demonstrations
- Capture product shots
- Film delivery scenarios

### Post-Production (1-2 weeks)
- Edit footage
- Add graphics and overlays
- Record voiceover
- Add music and sound effects
- Color grading
- Final review and revisions

### Distribution
- Upload to YouTube
- Create social media cuts
- Set up advertising campaigns
- Website integration
- App store preview videos
